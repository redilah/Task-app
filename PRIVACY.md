# Privacy Policy for Puncak

**Last Updated:** August 10, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
The Puncak app is designed to help you with monthly recaps and task management. We prioritize your privacy and data security. 

## 2. Local Storage
All your sensitive transaction and task data is stored securely **locally** on your device. We do not transmit your personal notes, tasks, or transaction details to our servers.

## 3. Telemetry and Analytics
We may use standard telemetry tools (like Firebase) to collect anonymous usage data and crash reports. This helps us improve the stability and performance of the app. This data does not include personally identifiable information.

## 4. Permissions
The app may request internet permission solely for fetching updates (like in-app updates via GitHub/Netlify) and sending anonymous crash reports.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
