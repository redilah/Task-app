# Privacy Policy for Puncak

**Effective Date:** August 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 10, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Puncak ("the App") is a personal monthly recap and task management application built for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about our data practices.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Data You Create
All tasks, notes, monthly recaps, and transaction records you enter into the App are stored **exclusively on your device** using local storage (localStorage / IndexedDB). This data is never transmitted to any external server.

### 2.2 Anonymous Analytics & Crash Reports
We may use **Google Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to collect:
- Anonymous usage statistics (e.g., which features are used most)
- Crash reports and performance diagnostics

This data is **anonymized** and does **not** contain any of your personal tasks, notes, financial data, or personally identifiable information (PII).

> For more information on how Google processes this data, please see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Data We Do NOT Collect

To be completely transparent, the App does **not**:
- Require any user account, login, or registration
- Collect your name, email address, phone number, or location
- Access your contacts, camera, microphone, or files
- Display advertisements or use ad-tracking SDKs
- Sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties
- Sync or upload your personal data to any cloud service

## 4. Permissions

The App requests only the following permission:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **Internet Access** (`android.permission.INTERNET`) | Required for checking in-app updates (via GitHub/Netlify) and sending anonymous crash reports via Firebase. |

No other sensitive permissions are requested.

## 5. Data Storage & Security

- All personal data (tasks, recaps, transactions) is stored **locally on your device only**.
- There is **no cloud backup or synchronization** — your data stays on your phone.
- Data is protected by your device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion

- Your data persists on your device as long as the App is installed.
- **To delete all your data:** simply uninstall the App or clear the App's data through your device's Settings → Apps → Puncak → Clear Data.
- We do not retain any copy of your personal data on any server.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect PII from any user, no special data from minors is at risk.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The App may utilize the following third-party services:

| Service | Provider | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---------|----------|---------|----------------|
| Firebase Analytics | Google LLC | Anonymous usage analytics | [Link](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) |
| Firebase Crashlytics | Google LLC | Crash reporting & diagnostics | [Link](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) |

These services may collect anonymous device identifiers and usage patterns in accordance with their own privacy policies.
